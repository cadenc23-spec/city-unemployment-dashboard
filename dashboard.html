Definitions and limitations

Mean in selection is the arithmetic mean of all displayed city-year rows. The 2025 mean uses selected rows from 2025 only; when 2025 is not selected, it is unavailable. City low and high are the lowest and highest means among cities in the current selection, averaged across the selected years.

Rows with missing or nonnumeric values would be rejected and reported by the page; the supplied CSV contains no missing values. The dataset has 250 unique city-year observations, with one record per city per year across 50 cities and 2021–2025. 2025 rates are 11-month averages because October data were not collected during the federal shutdown.
